# **Project Overview: ChaloBabshaKori.com**

**ChaloBabshaKori.com** is a business management and digital commerce platform designed to help entrepreneurs, small business owners, retailers, wholesalers, and growing companies manage their businesses more efficiently.

The platform will provide a centralized place to manage products, sales, purchases, inventory, customers, suppliers, payments, and business reports. Its primary goal is to reduce manual work, improve business visibility, and help business owners make better decisions.

The name "Chalo Babsha Kori" (চলো ব্যবসা করি) reflects the idea of making business easier, more accessible, and more organized for everyone.

## **1. Project Vision**

To build an easy-to-use, affordable, and scalable digital platform that enables business owners to operate, monitor, and grow their businesses from anywhere.

The platform will initially focus on the needs of Bangladeshi businesses, with support for Bangla and English, local currency (BDT), and common local business practices.

## **2. Problem Statement**

Many small and medium-sized businesses still rely on notebooks, spreadsheets, and disconnected software to manage their daily operations.

This creates several challenges:

- Difficulty tracking daily sales, purchases, and expenses.
- Poor visibility into available stock and inventory movement.
- Problems managing customer dues and supplier payments.
- Time-consuming preparation of financial and business reports.
- Limited access to business information when the owner is away.
- Difficulty expanding from a single shop to multiple branches.

ChaloBabshaKori.com aims to solve these problems through a centralized business management system.

## **3. Proposed Solution**

The platform will offer a web-based business management application where each business can maintain its own digital workspace.

**Business Dashboard**

A real-time overview of sales, expenses, profit, stock, and outstanding payments.

**Inventory and Product Management**

Track products, stock levels, purchases, suppliers, and low-stock alerts.

**Sales and Customer Management**

Create invoices, record sales, manage customer information, and track outstanding dues.

**Financial Reports and Analytics**

Understand revenue, expenses, gross profit, cash flow, and business performance.

## **4. Target Users**

The platform will be useful for:

| Business type         | Main use case                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Retail shops          | Daily sales, inventory, and customer dues                    |
| Wholesalers           | Bulk sales, purchase records, and supplier management        |
| Small manufacturers   | Raw materials, production stock, and finished goods          |
| E-commerce businesses | Orders, product stock, and customer management               |
| Service businesses    | Service invoices, expenses, and payment tracking             |
| Growing companies     | Employee access, multiple branches, and consolidated reports |

## **5. Core Features**

The project can be divided into the following functional modules.

**Business and User Management**

Business registration, user authentication, roles, permissions, and business profile settings.

**Product and Inventory Management**

Product categories, SKU/barcodes, stock adjustments, purchase history, and inventory valuation.

**Sales, Purchases, and Invoicing**

Sales invoices, purchase bills, discounts, returns, taxes, and payment records.

**Accounts and Financial Tracking**

Cash and bank transactions, customer receivables, supplier payables, and expense tracking.

**Reports and Business Insights**

Daily, monthly, and yearly reports with sales trends, profit analysis, and stock summaries.

## **6. Example Business Workflow**

Consider a retailer who sells products through a physical shop.

1. The business owner registers on ChaloBabshaKori.com and creates a business profile.
2. The owner adds products, their purchase prices, selling prices, and opening stock.
3. A supplier delivers 100 units of a product. The owner records the purchase, and the system updates inventory.
4. A customer purchases 5 units. The owner creates a sales invoice, records the payment, and the system reduces stock automatically.
5. The owner views the dashboard to see total sales, expenses, available stock, and outstanding customer dues.

This workflow demonstrates the platform's main value: connecting everyday business activities to accurate, accessible business information.

## **7. Technology and Architecture**

Since the project is intended to support multiple businesses and potentially grow into a larger platform, a modular architecture is a suitable starting point.

A possible technology stack:

| Layer             | Technology                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| Frontend          | React.js or Next.js       |
| Backend           | Java 17+ with Spring Boot |
| Database          | PostgreSQL                |
| Authentication    | Spring Security with JWT  |
| API documentation | OpenAPI / Swagger         |
| Deployment        | Docker and cloud hosting  |

A modular monolith is recommended for the initial version. It keeps development and deployment manageable while allowing business modules to be separated into microservices later if the platform grows.

## **9. Business Model**

ChaloBabshaKori.com can operate as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

- Free plan: Basic inventory and sales management for small businesses.
- Premium plan: Advanced reports, higher usage limits, and additional business features.
- Business plan: Multiple branches, employee management, and advanced access controls.

The pricing and feature limits can be finalized after validating the needs of actual business owners.

## **10. Expected Outcome**

The completed platform should help business owners:

- Reduce manual record-keeping and calculation errors.
- Monitor business performance from a single dashboard.
- Maintain better control over stock and payments.
- Make informed purchasing and sales decisions.
- Grow their businesses without needing complicated enterprise software.

**In summary:** ChaloBabshaKori.com is envisioned as an all-in-one digital business management platform that makes it easier for entrepreneurs and business owners to manage, monitor, and grow their businesses.

The strongest starting point is a simple inventory and sales management system for small retailers, with a foundation that can later support wholesalers, service businesses, and larger companies.
